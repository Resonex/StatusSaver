What it actually does

Built around one problem: statuses vanish fast.

Every feature exists to buy you time, or to stay out of your way once you have it.

◐

Live countdown badges

Every photo and video shows exactly how long it has left. Hours for what's still live on WhatsApp, days for what Status Saver has already kept safe.

✦

Keeps what WhatsApp deletes

Set your own retention window, from 1 to 30 days. Statuses WhatsApp has already removed stay browsable and saveable until your window closes.

◈

Two accounts, one grid

Grant WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business separately. Everything merges into one newest-first view, split cleanly into Images and Videos.

◎

Real-time watch mode

An optional background check catches new statuses within seconds and notifies you with a thumbnail. No need to keep the app open.

☆

Favorites and search

Star anything worth keeping longer. Instant filename search across everything you've seen, kept, or already saved.

◑

Zero data leaves your phone

No accounts, no cloud, no analytics. Every status, favorite, and setting lives in local storage you control.

Setup

Three taps, no file browsing required.

01

Grant folder access

Tap "WhatsApp." Your file picker opens already inside the right folder. Just tap "Use this folder."

02

Browse with a countdown

Images and videos load newest first, each one labeled with exactly how long it has left.

03

Save on your schedule

Select one or select all. Save now, or trust the countdown and come back before it runs out.
